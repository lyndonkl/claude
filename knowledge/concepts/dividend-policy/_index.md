## How these concepts connect

The area has three layers. **Foundations.** `dividend-decision-sequence` frames payout as the residual of the investment and financing decisions. `dividend-empirical-facts`, `dividend-payout-and-yield-measures` and `dividend-life-cycle` establish how firms actually behave, how policy is measured, and what payout is normal at each stage of a firm's life. **Theory.** `three-schools-of-dividend-thought` poses the value question and names the assumptions that decide the answer; `ex-dividend-day-and-dividend-capture` supplies the tax evidence and the arbitrage; `bad-reasons-for-paying-dividends`, `clientele-effect`, `dividend-signaling` and `dividend-wealth-transfer` sort the motives into fallacies and defensible reasons. **Assessment — the working toolkit.** Compute capacity with `fcfe-potential-dividends`, or `fcfe-for-banks` for financial firms. Measure what was actually returned with `cash-returned-dividends-and-buybacks`. Run the comparison and score management trust in `cash-trust-assessment` (the dividends.xls model). Convert the two verdicts into a recommendation with `dividend-matrix`. Cross-check against the cross-section using `peer-group-payout-analysis` and `market-regression-payout-prediction`. Project forward with `payout-forecasting`, quantify the repurchase alternative with `buyback-value-and-eps-effect`, and execute any change through `managing-dividend-changes`.

An analyst working a real company proceeds in roughly this order. (1) Measure the firm's payout ratio and yield, and place it in its life cycle and its region. (2) Compute FCFE over 5 to 10 years under all three debt assumptions, and compare it with dividends plus buybacks. (3) Score management trust from ROE versus cost of equity and Jensen's alpha, then read the quadrant of the dividend matrix. (4) Sanity-check against peers and the market regression, always adding buybacks back before drawing a conclusion. (5) Forecast FCFE and buyback capacity for the next five years. (6) If a change is warranted, weigh clientele, contractual and signaling constraints, choose between dividends and buybacks, and frame any cut around the investment story.
