## How these concepts connect, and the order to use them

Start at the framing layer. `role-of-accounting-and-three-statements` says what accounting is for and gives you the six reconciliation ties that must hold before anything else is worth reading. `accounting-standards-gaap-ifrs` tells you which rulebook produced the filing and which presentation surprises to expect.

Then work statement by statement. On the income statement, `income-statement-structure` gives the ladder, and three concepts feed it: `accrual-accounting-and-revenue-recognition` for the top line, `expense-classification-and-depreciation` for the middle, and `non-operating-items-and-cross-holdings` for everything below operating income. `extraordinary-items-and-pro-forma-earnings` is the cleanup pass over the result. `segment-and-geographic-reporting` decomposes the cleaned statement by business and region.

On the balance sheet, `balance-sheet-views-and-asset-measurement` sets expectations about what the numbers mean, and three concepts handle the parts that most often mislead: `intangibles-and-goodwill`, `liabilities-debt-and-leases` and `shareholders-equity-book-value`. The debt concept is also the prerequisite for any working-capital work, because interest-bearing short-term borrowing must be reclassified first.

On the cash flow statement, `cash-flow-statement-structure` gives the three sections. Then work each: `cash-flow-from-operations-and-working-capital`, `investing-cash-flows-and-reinvestment`, `financing-cash-flows-and-cash-returned`. Those three assemble into `potential-dividends-fcfe`, which is the main output this area hands to valuation. `earnings-versus-cash-flows` is the cross-check that runs between the income statement and the cash flow statement.

Two concepts run across all three statements and should be applied as a lens, not a step. `life-cycle-patterns-in-financial-statements` tells you what normal looks like for a firm of this age; `life-cycle-financing-and-dividend-capacity` extends that to funding and payout. `sector-differences-in-financial-statements` tells you when the standard template does not apply at all — most sharply for banks.

Finish at `financial-balance-sheet` and `cash-flow-claim-types`. These two convert accounting output into valuation input: four boxes of value rather than book entries, and three claim types rather than legal labels. Everything upstream exists to make those two conversions honest.
